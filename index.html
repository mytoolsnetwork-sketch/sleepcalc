What Is a Sleep Calculator?

A sleep calculator helps you find the best time to go to bed or wake up based on your body's natural 90-minute sleep cycles. Instead of waking up groggy mid-cycle, you wake up at the end of one — feeling refreshed and alert. Our free sleep calculator does the math for you instantly.

How Sleep Cycles Work

Your sleep is divided into repeated 90-minute cycles, each containing four stages: light sleep, deep sleep, and REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep. Most adults need 5 to 6 complete cycles per night — that's roughly 7.5 to 9 hours of sleep. Waking up naturally at the end of a cycle means your brain has completed its restoration process.

The calculator also adds 14 minutes — the average time it takes most people to fall asleep — so your recommended bedtimes are accurate from the moment your head hits the pillow.

How Many Hours of Sleep Do You Need?

  • Teenagers (14–17): 8–10 hours (5–6 sleep cycles)
  • Adults (18–64): 7–9 hours (5–6 sleep cycles)
  • Older adults (65+): 7–8 hours (4–5 sleep cycles)

These are general guidelines — some people naturally feel best on slightly more or less sleep. The key is to wake up at the end of a complete cycle, whatever that looks like for you.

Tips for Better Sleep

  • Keep a consistent schedule — go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
  • Avoid screens before bed — blue light from phones and laptops suppresses melatonin production.
  • Keep your room cool and dark — the ideal sleep temperature is around 18°C (65°F).
  • Avoid caffeine after 2pm — caffeine has a half-life of about 5 hours and can disrupt your sleep cycles.
  • Wind down before bed — a relaxing routine signals to your brain that it's time to sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I go to sleep to wake up at 6am?

To wake up at 6:00am feeling refreshed, you should go to sleep at 8:46pm, 10:16pm, 11:46pm, or 1:16am — each option aligns with the end of a complete 90-minute sleep cycle.

Is 6 hours of sleep enough?

6 hours equals 4 sleep cycles, which is below the recommended amount for most adults. While some people function on less, most need 7.5 to 9 hours for optimal health, focus, and mood.

Why do I wake up tired even after 8 hours of sleep?

Waking up tired after a full night's sleep often means you woke up mid-cycle. Use the sleep calculator to align your alarm with the end of a complete cycle — even 7.5 hours timed correctly can feel better than 8 hours timed wrong.

What is REM sleep and why does it matter?

REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep is the stage where your brain consolidates memories, processes emotions, and restores cognitive function. It mostly occurs in the later cycles of the night — another reason why cutting sleep short can leave you feeling foggy.
