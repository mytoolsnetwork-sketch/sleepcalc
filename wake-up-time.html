What Is the Best Time to Wake Up?

The best time to wake up depends on when you fall asleep and how many complete 90-minute sleep cycles you complete. Waking up at the end of a cycle means your body has finished its natural rest phase — so you feel alert and refreshed instead of groggy.

How to Use This Wake-Up Time Calculator

  • Enter your bedtime — the time you plan to get into bed.
  • Add 14 minutes — the average time it takes to fall asleep (already included in our calculation).
  • Choose a wake-up time that aligns with 5 or 6 complete sleep cycles for the best rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I wake up if I go to sleep at 10 PM?

If you fall asleep at 10:00 PM, the best wake-up times are 5:14 AM (6 cycles = 9 hours) or 6:44 AM (5 cycles = 7.5 hours).

Is it better to wake up after 6 or 7 hours of sleep?

It depends on your sleep cycles. 7.5 hours (5 cycles) is usually better than 7 hours because 7.5 hours aligns with a complete cycle — meaning you wake up naturally at the end of REM sleep rather than mid-cycle.

What happens if I wake up mid-cycle?

Waking up in the middle of a sleep cycle — especially during deep sleep — causes sleep inertia, that groggy, disoriented feeling that can last up to an hour. Timing your alarm to the end of a cycle eliminates this.

Should I use this calculator every night?

Ideally yes — especially if your bedtime varies. Consistent sleep and wake times are the best foundation for good sleep, but when your schedule shifts, this calculator helps you find the next best option.
