About SleepCalc

SleepCalc is a free, simple tool built around one idea: good sleep isn't just about how many hours you get — it's about when you wake up relative to your natural sleep cycles.

Most adults move through 90-minute sleep cycles each night, transitioning through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep. Waking up at the end of a complete cycle means your body has finished its natural rest process, leaving you feeling refreshed instead of groggy. Waking up in the middle of a cycle — especially during deep sleep — causes that disoriented, heavy feeling known as sleep inertia.

SleepCalc takes the guesswork out of this. Whether you know your bedtime or your wake-up time, our calculators work backward or forward through 90-minute cycles to suggest the times that align with your body's natural rhythm.

🎯 Our Mission

To make sleep science simple and actionable. No medical jargon, no apps to download, no sign-up required — just a fast, free tool that gives you a clear answer based on real sleep science.

🔬 The Science Behind It

Our calculations are based on the well-established 90-minute sleep cycle model used widely in sleep research. We also factor in the average 14 minutes it takes most people to fall asleep, so our recommendations reflect real-world sleep timing rather than theoretical numbers.

🛠️ What We Offer

Beyond our main sleep calculator, we've built dedicated tools for specific needs: a bedtime calculator, a wake-up time calculator, age-based sleep recommendations, a kids' sleep calculator, and a nap calculator — all free, all instant, all based on the same sleep cycle science.
